Economic benefits

40% time saved ≈ 3 FTEs back.

Translate the time we lose today into people-equivalents — and the case writes itself.

The equation

40% of engagement hours, recovered across the book, equals the capacity of roughly three staff redirected from mechanical work to client-value work.
Margin improvement
Cost-reimbursement engagements stop leaking hours that can't be billed.

~3 FTEs available for new engagements, review work, or advisory — without hiring.
Client experience
Less chasing, faster billing, cleaner audit packages — fewer escalations to manager and MD.
Method: 40% time-loss estimate × current NFP-engagement hours ÷ FTE annual hours. Conservative — assumes no growth in NFP book.
